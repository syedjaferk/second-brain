🚀 Episode 2 - Core Linux Prerequisites: Processes, PIDs, Mounts, Signals

Docker isn't magic — it's built directly on top of core Linux 
concepts. Before we go deeper into containers, this session breaks 
down the Linux fundamentals that make Docker (and later, Kubernetes) 
actually work under the hood.

In this video, we cover:
✅ What a Process is and how the OS manages it
✅ Understanding PID (Process ID) and the process tree
✅ Mounts basics — how filesystems get attached and why it matters 
   for containers
✅ Signals — how processes communicate and get terminated (SIGKILL, 
   SIGTERM, etc.)
✅ Why these concepts are the foundation for container isolation

This is Episode 2 of our Kube Engineering series — understand these 
Linux basics now, and Docker's internals will make a lot more sense 
later.
