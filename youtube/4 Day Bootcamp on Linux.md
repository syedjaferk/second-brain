Welcome back to Day 2 of our 4-Day Linux & Bash Bootcamp! 🚀 Today we go deeper into the Linux filesystem structure and learn the essential commands for viewing and reading file contents — a must-know skill for any Linux user, sysadmin, or developer.

**🐧 Linux & Bash Bootcamp | Day 3 – Disk Usage, Processes & File Searching**

Welcome to Day 3 of our 4-Day Linux & Bash Bootcamp! 🚀 Today's session is all about monitoring your system and finding exactly what you need — critical skills for managing servers, debugging issues, and keeping your system healthy.

**📌 What We Covered Today:**  
✅ **Disk Usage** – `df` (check disk space on filesystems) and `du` (check size of files/directories)  
✅ **Process Management** – `ps` (view running processes) and `top`/`htop` (real-time system monitoring)  
✅ Killing/managing processes with `kill` and understanding Process IDs (PID)  
✅ **File Searching** – `find` (locate files by name, type, size, date) and `grep` (search inside file contents)  
✅ Combining commands with pipes (`|`) for powerful one-liners

By the end of this video, you'll be able to check how much disk space you have, monitor what's running on your system, and quickly locate any file or piece of text — skills every Linux user and sysadmin relies on daily.
