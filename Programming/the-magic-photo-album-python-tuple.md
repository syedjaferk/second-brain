They open their magical photo album and start adding new snapshots.</p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Let’s create snapshots:</strong></p><pre class="wp-block-syntaxhighlighter-code"># Snapshots of the family's adventuresooty_adventure = ("Ooty", "2024-07-01", "Visited Botanical Garden")munnar_adventure = ("Munnar", "2023-12-15", "Saw Nilgiri Tahr in Eravikulam national park")manjolai_adventure = ("Manjolai", "2023-05-21", "Enjoyed hill stations")photo_album = [ooty_adventure, munnar_adventure, manjolai_adventure]</pre><h2 class="wp-block-heading">Noon: Recalling Memories</h2><p class="wp-block-paragraph">At noon, the family gathers around the table and starts recalling details of their Ooty trip.</p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Accessing Elements:</strong></p><pre class="wp-block-syntaxhighlighter-code"># Accessing elements in a tuplelocation = ooty_adventure[0]date = ooty_adventure[1]description = ooty_adventure[2]print(f"Location: {location}, Date: {date}, Description: {description}")# output: Location: Ooty, Date: 2024-07-01, Description: Visited Botanical Garden</pre><p class="wp-block-paragraph"></p><h2 class="wp-block-heading">Afternoon: Sharing Stories</h2><p class="wp-block-paragraph">In the afternoon, they decide to share the details of their Munnar trip by unpacking the snapshot into separate parts.</p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Unpacking Tuples:</strong></p><pre class="wp-block-syntaxhighlighter-code"># Unpacking tuplelocation, date, description = munnar_adventureprint(f"Location: {location}, Date: {date}, Description: {description}")</pre><p class="wp-block-paragraph">After some time, They decided to change the values of an ooty adventure</p><pre class="wp-block-syntaxhighlighter-code">ooty_adventure[2] = "Visited Doddabeta Peak"</pre><figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img alt="" class="wp-image-743" data-recalc-dims="1" decoding="async" height="315" loading="lazy" src="https://i0.wp.com/parottasalna.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/image.png?resize=1816%2C315&amp;ssl=1" width="1816"/></figure><p class="wp-block-paragraph">Tuples in Python are immutable, meaning that once a tuple is created, its elements cannot be changed. </p><p class="wp-block-paragraph">However, there are some workarounds to “change” values within a tuple by converting it to a mutable data type, such as a list, making the change, and then converting it back to a tuple.</p><h2 class="wp-block-heading">Evening: Combining Adventures</h2><p class="wp-block-paragraph">As evening approaches, the family feels nostalgic and decides to combine the Paris and Kenya adventures into one big story.</p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Concatenating Tuples:</strong></p><pre class="wp-block-syntaxhighlighter-code"># Concatenating tuplescombined_adventure = ooty_adventure + munnar_adventureprint(combined_adventure)</pre><h2 class="wp-block-heading">Night: Reliving Special Moments</h2><p class="wp-block-paragraph">Before going to bed, they want to relive the Japan adventure over and over again, emphasizing how special it was.</p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Repeating Tuples:</strong></p><pre class="wp-block-syntaxhighlighter-code"># Repeating tuplesrepeated_adventure = manjolai_adventure * 2print(repeated_adventure)</pre><h2 class="wp-block-heading">Midnight: Checking the Album</h2><p class="wp-block-paragraph">Late at night, they decide to check if their favorite adventures are still part of their magical photo album.</p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Checking Membership:</strong></p><pre class="wp-block-syntaxhighlighter-code"># Checking membershipis_ooty_in_album = ooty_adventure in photo_albumis_munnar_in_album = munnar_adventure in photo_albumprint(f"Is the Ooty adventure in the album? {is_ooty_in_album}")print(f"Is the Munnar adventure in the album? {is_munnar_in_album}")</pre><h2 class="wp-block-heading">Counting Their Adventures</h2><p class="wp-block-paragraph">They also count the number of adventures they have documented in the album so far.</p><pre class="wp-block-syntaxhighlighter-code"># Finding length of the tuplenumber_of_adventures = len(photo_album)print(f"Number of adventures in the album: {number_of_adventures}")# Output: Number of adventures in the album: 3</pre><p class="wp-block-paragraph">And then they had a good sleep …</p>
