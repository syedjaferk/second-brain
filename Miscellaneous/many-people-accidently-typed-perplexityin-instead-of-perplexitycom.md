Google bought the .in domain for Perplexity. Huge move!</p></blockquote><p class="wp-block-paragraph"></p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"></p><h2 class="wp-block-heading">🔍 A Quick Whois Check</h2><p class="wp-block-paragraph">I compared the Whois records of <strong>perplexity.in</strong> and <strong>google.com</strong>.</p><ul class="wp-block-list"><li><strong>perplexity.in</strong> → Registered through <em>GoDaddy.com</em>, uses <em>standard GoDaddy nameservers</em>.</li><li><strong>google.com</strong> → Registered with Google’s own domain infrastructure and Google-managed nameservers.</li></ul><p class="wp-block-paragraph">If Google truly purchased <strong>perplexity.in</strong>, why would they rely on GoDaddy’s nameservers instead of their own?<br/><br/>Exactly they wouldn’t.</p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"><br/>So what’s really happening?<br/><br/>💡 The Reality</p><p class="wp-block-paragraph">Someone just a regular person has purchased <strong>perplexity.in</strong>, most likely hoping that Perplexity AI might someday want the domain and be willing to buy it for a premium.<br/></p><p class="wp-block-paragraph">It’s simple business, not a secret Google acquisition.</p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"><br/>Before copy pasting viral messages, always ask:</p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"><br/>What proof do we have?</p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"><br/>Is this technically true?</p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"></p><h2 class="wp-block-heading">✅ Key Takeaways</h2><ol class="wp-block-list"><li><strong>Domain flipping works.</strong><br/>If a company hasn’t secured obvious domain variations, someone else can buy them and later sell them for profit.</li><li><strong>Always verify claims.</strong><br/>A 10-second Whois lookup can stop misinformation from spreading.</li></ol><p class="wp-block-paragraph"></p><p class="wp-block-paragraph">Staying curious is good. Staying <em>informed</em> is better.</p>
