<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Setting the Scene</h3><p class="wp-block-paragraph">Once upon a time in the bustling town of <strong>Pettai</strong>, there was a famous restaurant called <strong>ArRahman Hotel</strong>. This wasn’t just any ordinary restaurant; it had a <strong>Magic Kitchen</strong>. The Magic Kitchen had the ability to create different kinds of meals almost instantly, without any confusion or delay. Let’s explore how the restaurant achieved this magical feat.</p><figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img alt="" class="wp-image-891" data-recalc-dims="1" decoding="async" height="478" loading="lazy" src="https://i0.wp.com/parottasalna.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/image-17.png?resize=1130%2C478&amp;ssl=1" width="1130"/></figure><h3 class="wp-block-heading">The Magic Kitchen (Docker Engine)</h3><p class="wp-block-paragraph">The heart of the restaurant was the Magic Kitchen, which had a unique power. Just like the <strong>Docker Engine</strong>, it could take orders and prepare meals consistently and efficiently. The kitchen knew how to handle everything without mixing ingredients or creating chaos, no matter how many orders came in.</p><p class="wp-block-paragraph">The Docker engine comprises of <strong>dockerd (docker daemon), Docker Client (docker), REST Api, Container Runtime, Image Management, Networking, Volume management, Security mechanisms, Plugins &amp; Extensions.</strong></p><figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img alt="" class="wp-image-898" data-recalc-dims="1" decoding="async" height="555" loading="lazy" src="https://i0.wp.com/parottasalna.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/image-18.png?resize=1226%2C555&amp;ssl=1" width="1226"/></figure><h3 class="wp-block-heading">The Recipe Book (Docker Images)</h3><p class="wp-block-paragraph">In the Magic Kitchen, the chefs relied on a special <strong>Recipe Book</strong>. This Recipe Book contained detailed instructions on how to prepare each dish. These instructions are like <strong>Docker Images</strong>. </p><p class="wp-block-paragraph">Each image is a <strong>blueprint</strong> for creating a meal (or application), containing everything needed to cook it up: the ingredients, the cooking method, and any special tools required.</p><figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img alt="" class="wp-image-900" data-recalc-dims="1" decoding="async" height="555" loading="lazy" src="https://i0.wp.com/parottasalna.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/image-19.png?resize=1226%2C555&amp;ssl=1" width="1226"/></figure><h3 class="wp-block-heading">The Prepped Ingredients – (Docker Containers)</h3><p class="wp-block-paragraph">When an order came in, the chefs would use the Recipe Book to gather prepped ingredients from the pantry. These ingredients are similar to <strong>Docker Containers</strong>. Containers are the actual meals created from the recipes, ready to be served. They are isolated from each other, ensuring that the flavors (or code) don’t mix and cause unwanted results.</p><figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img alt="" class="wp-image-902" data-recalc-dims="1" decoding="async" height="421" loading="lazy" src="https://i0.wp.com/parottasalna.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/image-20.png?resize=1159%2C421&amp;ssl=1" width="1159"/></figure><h3 class="wp-block-heading">The Pantry (Docker Registry)</h3><p class="wp-block-paragraph">The Magic Kitchen had a vast pantry where all the ingredients were stored. This pantry represents the <strong>Docker Registry</strong>. It holds all the images (recipes) that the kitchen might need, ready to be pulled and used whenever required. The registry ensures that every meal is prepared with the correct ingredients.</p><figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img alt="" class="wp-image-903" data-recalc-dims="1" decoding="async" height="488" loading="lazy" src="https://i0.wp.com/parottasalna.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/image-21.png?resize=1266%2C488&amp;ssl=1" width="1266"/></figure><h3 class="wp-block-heading">The Chefs (Docker Daemon)</h3><p class="wp-block-paragraph">The chefs in the Magic Kitchen are like the <strong>Docker Daemon</strong>. They are responsible for reading the recipes, pulling the necessary ingredients from the pantry, and preparing the meals in containers. They handle the entire process, ensuring everything runs smoothly and efficiently.</p><figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img alt="" class="wp-image-905" data-recalc-dims="1" decoding="async" height="396" loading="lazy" src="https://i0.wp.com/parottasalna.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/image-22.png?resize=613%2C396&amp;ssl=1" width="613"/></figure><h3 class="wp-block-heading">The Customers (Developers and Users)</h3><p class="wp-block-paragraph">The patrons of ArRahman’s are the <strong>Developers and Users</strong>. They come to the restaurant with various demands, and the kitchen satisfies them quickly and reliably. Developers can focus on creating new recipes without worrying about the cooking process, while users enjoy consistent and delicious meals every time.</p><h3 class="wp-block-heading">The Special Dining Areas (Docker Networks and Volumes)</h3><p class="wp-block-paragraph">ArRahman also had special dining areas with unique themes and settings, ensuring each meal was experienced perfectly. These are like <strong>Docker Networks and Volumes</strong>. Networks allow different containers to communicate, much like guests chatting over dinner. Volumes store data persistently, like special utensils or decor kept for regular guests.</p><figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img alt="" class="wp-image-906" data-recalc-dims="1" decoding="async" height="278" loading="lazy" src="https://i0.wp.com/parottasalna.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/image-23.png?resize=1285%2C278&amp;ssl=1" width="1285"/></figure><p class="wp-block-paragraph">So atlast the docker architecture is, </p><figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img alt="" class="wp-image-907" data-recalc-dims="1" decoding="async" height="908" loading="lazy" src="https://i0.wp.com/parottasalna.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/image-24.png?resize=771%2C908&amp;ssl=1" width="771"/></figure>
