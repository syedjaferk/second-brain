<p class="wp-block-paragraph"></p><p class="wp-block-paragraph">Many Thanks to <a href="https://goinggnu.wordpress.com/2024/09/08/closing-notes-on-2-months-online-python-course/">Shrini for documenting it last year</a>. This serves as a good reference to improve my skills. Hope it will help many. </p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"></p><h2 class="wp-block-heading">📢 <strong>What Participants </strong>wanted to improve</h2><p class="wp-block-paragraph">🚶‍♂️ Go a bit slower so that everyone can understand clearly without feeling rushed.</p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"><br/>📚 Provide more basics and examples to make learning easier for beginners.</p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"><br/>🖥️ Spend the first week explaining programming basics so that newcomers don’t feel lost.</p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"><br/>📊 Teach flowcharting methods to help participants understand the logic behind coding.</p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"><br/>🕹️ Try teaching Scratch as an interactive way to introduce programming concepts.</p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"><br/>🗓️ Offer weekend batches for those who prefer learning on weekends.</p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"><br/>🗣️ Encourage more conversations so that participants can actively engage in discussions.</p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"><br/>👥 Create sub-groups to allow participants to collaborate and support each other.</p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"><br/>🎉 Get “cheerleaders” within the team to make the classes more fun and interactive.</p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"><br/>📢 Increase promotion efforts to reach a wider audience and get more participants.</p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"><br/>🔍 Provide better examples to make concepts easier to grasp.</p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"><br/>❓ Conduct more Q&amp;A sessions so participants can ask and clarify their doubts.</p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"><br/>🎙️ Ensure that each participant gets a chance to speak and express their thoughts.</p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"><br/>📹 Showing your face in videos can help in building a more personal connection with the learners.</p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"><br/>🏆 Organize mini-hackathons to provide hands-on experience and encourage practical learning.</p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"><br/>🔗 Foster more interactions and connections between participants to build a strong learning community.</p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"><br/>✍️ Encourage participants to write blogs daily to document their learning and share insights.</p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"><br/>🎤 Motivate participants to give talks in class and other communities to build confidence.<br/><br/></p><h2 class="wp-block-heading">📝 <strong>Other Learnings &amp; Suggestions</strong></h2><p class="wp-block-paragraph">📵 Avoid creating WhatsApp groups for communication, as the 1024 member limit makes it difficult to manage multiple groups.</p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"><br/>✉️ Telegram works fine for now, but explore using mailing lists as an alternative for structured discussions.</p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"><br/>🔕 Mute groups when necessary to prevent unnecessary messages like “Hi, Hello, Good Morning.”</p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"><br/>📢 Teach participants how to join mailing lists like ChennaiPy and KanchiLUG and guide them on asking questions in forums like <a href="https://forums.tamillinuxcommunity.org/">Tamil Linux Community</a>.</p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"><br/>📝 Show participants how to create a free blog on platforms like dev.to or WordPress to share their learning journey.</p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"><br/>🛠️ Avoid spending too much time explaining everything in-depth, as participants should start coding a small project by the 5th or 6th class.</p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"><br/>📌 Present topics as solutions to project ideas or real-world problem statements instead of just theory.</p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"><br/>👤 Encourage using names when addressing people, rather than calling them “Sir” or “Madam,” to maintain an equal and friendly learning environment.</p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"><br/>💸 Zoom is costly, and since only around 50 people complete the training, consider alternatives like Jitsi or Google Meet for better cost-effectiveness.</p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"></p><p class="wp-block-paragraph">Will try to incorporate these learnings in our <a href="https://calendar.parottasalna.com/">upcoming sessions.</a></p><p class="wp-block-paragraph">🚀 Let’s make this learning experience engaging, interactive, and impactful! 🎯</p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"></p>
