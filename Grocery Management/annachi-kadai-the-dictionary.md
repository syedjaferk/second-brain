<p class="wp-block-paragraph">In a vibrant town in Tamil Nadu, there is a popular grocery store called Annachi Kadai. This store is always bustling with fresh deliveries of items. </p><p class="wp-block-paragraph">The store owner, Pandian, uses a special inventory system to track the products. This system functions like a dictionary in Python, where each item is labeled with its name, and the quantity available is recorded.<br/><br/></p><figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter size-large is-resized"><img alt="" class="wp-image-755" data-recalc-dims="1" decoding="async" height="683" loading="lazy" src="https://i0.wp.com/parottasalna.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/image-2.png?resize=1024%2C683&amp;ssl=1" style="width:628px;height:auto" width="1024"/></figure><h2 class="wp-block-heading">Morning: Delivering Items to the Store</h2><p class="wp-block-paragraph">One bright morning, a new delivery truck arrives at the grocery store, packed with fresh items. Pandian records these new items in his inventory list.</p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Creating and Updating the Inventory:</strong></p><pre class="wp-block-syntaxhighlighter-code"># Initial delivery of items to the storeinventory = {    "apples": 20,    "bananas": 30,    "carrots": 15,    "milk": 10}print("Initial Inventory:", inventory)# Output: Initial Inventory: {'apples': 20, 'bananas': 30, 'carrots': 15, 'milk': 10}</pre><h2 class="wp-block-heading">Noon: Additional Deliveries</h2><p class="wp-block-paragraph">As the day progresses, more deliveries arrive with additional items that need to be added to the inventory. Pandian updates the system with these new arrivals.<br/><br/><strong>Adding New Items to the Inventory:</strong></p><pre class="wp-block-syntaxhighlighter-code"># Adding more items from the deliveryinventory["bread"] = 25inventory["eggs"] = 50print("Updated Inventory:", inventory)# Output: Updated Inventory: {'apples': 20, 'bananas': 30, 'carrots': 15, 'milk': 10, 'bread': 25, 'eggs': 50}</pre><h2 class="wp-block-heading">Afternoon: Stocking the Shelves</h2><p class="wp-block-paragraph">In the afternoon, Pandian notices that some items are running low and restocks them by updating the quantities in the inventory system.</p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Updating Quantities:</strong></p><pre class="wp-block-syntaxhighlighter-code"># Updating item quantities after restocking shelvesinventory["apples"] += 10  # 10 more apples addedinventory["milk"] += 5     # 5 more bottles of milk addedprint("Inventory after Restocking:", inventory)# Output: Inventory after Restocking: {'apples': 30, 'bananas': 30, 'carrots': 15, 'milk': 15, 'bread': 25, 'eggs': 50}</pre><h2 class="wp-block-heading">Evening: Removing Sold-Out Items</h2><p class="wp-block-paragraph">As evening falls, some items are sold out, and Pandian needs to remove them from the inventory to reflect their unavailability.</p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Removing Items from the Inventory:</strong></p><pre class="wp-block-syntaxhighlighter-code"># Removing sold-out itemsdel inventory["carrots"]print("Inventory after Removal:", inventory)# Output: Inventory after Removal: {'apples': 30, 'bananas': 30, 'milk': 15, 'bread': 25, 'eggs': 50}</pre><h2 class="wp-block-heading">Night: Checking Inventory</h2><p class="wp-block-paragraph">Before closing the store, Pandian checks the inventory to ensure that all items are accurately recorded and none are missing.</p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Checking for Items:</strong></p><pre class="wp-block-syntaxhighlighter-code"># Checking if specific items are in the inventoryis_bananas_in_stock = "bananas" in inventoryis_oranges_in_stock = "oranges" in inventoryprint(f"Are bananas in stock? {is_bananas_in_stock}")print(f"Are oranges in stock? {is_oranges_in_stock}")# Output: Are bananas in stock? True# Output: Are oranges in stock? False</pre><h2 class="wp-block-heading">Midnight: Reviewing Inventory</h2><p class="wp-block-paragraph">After a busy day, Pandian reviews the entire inventory to ensure all deliveries and sales are accurately recorded.<br/></p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Iterating Over the Inventory:</strong></p><pre class="wp-block-syntaxhighlighter-code"># Reviewing the final inventoryfor item, quantity in inventory.items():    print(f"Item: {item}, Quantity: {quantity}")# Output:# Item: apples, Quantity: 30# Item: bananas, Quantity: 30# Item: milk, Quantity: 15# Item: bread, Quantity: 25# Item: eggs, Quantity: 50</pre><p class="wp-block-paragraph"></p>
