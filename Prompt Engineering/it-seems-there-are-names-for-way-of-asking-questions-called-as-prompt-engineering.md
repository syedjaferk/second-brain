<p class="wp-block-paragraph"></p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Prompting</strong> is the process of giving <strong>specific instructions, questions, or examples</strong> to a Large Language Model (LLM) like ChatGPT to <strong>guide its output</strong> toward the desired goal.</p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"></p><p class="wp-block-paragraph">In other words, its about how you are making conversation with an LLM. </p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"></p><h2 class="wp-block-heading">Goal of Prompting</h2><ul class="wp-block-list"><li>To <strong>clarify the task</strong> for the AI.</li><li>To <strong>control the format, tone, and depth</strong> of the output.</li><li>To <strong>encourage reasoning</strong> or multi-step thinking.</li><li>To <strong>reduce hallucination</strong> by narrowing the context.</li></ul><p class="wp-block-paragraph"></p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"></p><figure class="wp-block-table"><table class="has-fixed-layout"><thead><tr><th>Type</th><th>Description</th><th>Example</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td><strong>Zero-Shot</strong></td><td>No examples given</td><td>“Summarize this text.”</td></tr><tr><td><strong>One-Shot</strong></td><td>One example given</td><td>“Example: Dog → perro; Cat → ?”</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Few-Shot</strong></td><td>Multiple examples</td><td>“Translate the following… cat→chat, dog→chien…”</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Chain-of-Thought</strong></td><td>Ask to reason step by step</td><td>“Think step by step.”</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Role-Based</strong></td><td>Assign an identity</td><td>“You are a Python teacher.”</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Self-Reflexive</strong></td><td>Ask model to review itself</td><td>“Check and improve your answer.”</td></tr></tbody></table></figure><p class="wp-block-paragraph"></p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"></p><p class="wp-block-paragraph">Below are some examples , just for reference. I hope we are not moving to do a GridSearch to find which prompt works better ? Who Knows ???</p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"></p><h2 class="wp-block-heading">1. Zero Shot Prompting</h2><p class="wp-block-paragraph"></p><p class="wp-block-paragraph">Ask the model to perform a task <strong>without prior examples</strong>.</p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Examples</strong></p><ol class="wp-block-list"><li>“Write a poem about deep learning.”</li><li>“Summarize this paragraph in 2 lines.”</li><li>“Explain quantum computing in simple terms.”</li><li>“Generate a SQL query to find all customers who made purchases in the last month.”</li></ol><p class="wp-block-paragraph"></p><h2 class="wp-block-heading">2. One-Shot Prompting</h2><p class="wp-block-paragraph">Provide <strong>one example</strong> to show the expected format.</p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Examples</strong></p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"></p><ol class="wp-block-list"><li>Example<br/>Q: What is the capital of Japan?<br/>A: Tokyo<br/>Q: What is the capital of Italy? ➜ Output: Rome<br/></li><li>Example<br/>English → Spanish<br/>cat → gato<br/>dog → ? ➜ Output: perro<br/></li><li>Example<br/>Input: “Increase brightness”<br/>Output: “Adjusting display to +10% brightness”<br/>Input: “Lower volume” → ? ➜ Output: “Decreasing sound by 10%”</li></ol><p class="wp-block-paragraph"></p><h2 class="wp-block-heading">3. Few-Shot Prompting</h2><p class="wp-block-paragraph">Give <strong>multiple examples</strong> to teach the model a consistent pattern.</p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Examples</strong></p><ol class="wp-block-list"><li>Sentiment Classification<ul><li>“I love this movie!” → Positive“This is terrible.” → Negative“It’s okay, not great.” → Neutral“The acting was amazing!” → ?</li></ul>➜ Positive<br/></li><li>SQL Generation<ul><li>“List all employees.” → <code>SELECT * FROM employees;</code>“ List all products.” → <code>SELECT * FROM products;</code>“List all customers.” → ?</li></ul>➜ <code>SELECT * FROM customers;</code></li></ol><p class="wp-block-paragraph"></p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"></p><h2 class="wp-block-heading">4. Chain-of-Thought Prompting (CoT)</h2><p class="wp-block-paragraph">Ask the model to <strong>think step by step</strong> before answering.</p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Examples</strong></p><ol class="wp-block-list"><li>“If 4 pens cost ₹20, how much do 10 pens cost? Think step by step.”<br/>➜ Step: 1 pen = ₹5 → 10 × 5 = ₹50<br/></li><li>“Why does the sun appear red during sunset? Explain step by step.”<br/>➜ Step 1: Sunlight passes through thicker atmosphere → Step 2: Blue light scatters → Step 3: Red light reaches eyes.<br/></li><li>“You have 12 marbles, give 4 to a friend. How many left? Explain.”<br/>➜ Step: 12 − 4 = 8 marbles.</li></ol><p class="wp-block-paragraph"></p><h2 class="wp-block-heading">5. ReAct Prompting (Reason + Act)</h2><p class="wp-block-paragraph">Combine <strong>reasoning and action</strong> explicitly.</p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Examples</strong></p><ol class="wp-block-list"><li>“You’re a travel planner. Think about ideal locations for a short vacation from Chennai. Then list 3 options.”</li><li>“You are an SRE. Think about why the API latency is high. Then suggest 3 mitigation steps.”</li><li>“You are a data analyst. Think about how to visualize daily user logins. Then produce the Matplotlib code.”</li></ol><p class="wp-block-paragraph"></p><h2 class="wp-block-heading">6. Self-Consistency Prompting</h2><p class="wp-block-paragraph">Ask for <strong>multiple reasoning paths</strong> to get the most reliable answer.</p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Examples</strong></p><ol class="wp-block-list"><li>“What is 25% of 160? Give 3 reasoning paths and pick the best.”<br/>➜ All lead to 40.</li><li>“How many minutes in 3.5 hours? Solve in 2 ways and choose consistent.”<br/>➜ 3.5×60 = 210.</li><li>“If the train leaves at 3 PM and travels 2.5 hours, what time will it reach?”<br/>➜ 5:30 PM via all methods.</li></ol><p class="wp-block-paragraph"></p><h2 class="wp-block-heading">7. Socratic Prompting</h2><p class="wp-block-paragraph">Use a <strong>question-driven</strong> approach to guide understanding.</p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Examples:</strong></p><ol class="wp-block-list"><li>“What happens when we drop an object? Why? What is that force called?”</li><li>“If databases use indexes, what is their purpose? How do they help?”</li><li>“If a model overfits, what could that mean about its training data?”</li></ol><p class="wp-block-paragraph"></p><p class="wp-block-paragraph"></p>
